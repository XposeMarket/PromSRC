A

Why this works

This page imports the open-source ios-vibrator-pro-max polyfill from vibrator.dev. It turns trusted button taps and pointer movement into iOS Safari haptics.

B

What to expect

Haptics require a physical actuator. On iPhone, use Safari with system haptics enabled, tap the arm button first, then drag slowly. Desktop browsers show the trace but cannot physically vibrate.
