# Action Extraction and Commitment Evidence

Use this pass after the initial meeting-note extraction and before presenting action items as commitments.

## Extract only observable commitments

For every candidate action, record the exact supporting sentence or a short faithful quote, speaker/source, owner, deadline, dependency, and confidence. Distinguish:

- **Decision:** the group agreed to a direction.
- **Action:** someone committed to a concrete next step.
- **Follow-up:** work is needed but no owner or deadline was agreed.
- **Suggestion:** an idea that was discussed without commitment.

Do not convert wishes, questions, or polite possibilities into assigned tasks. If ownership is inferred from context, mark it as inferred and keep the original wording available.

## Required action fields

Use a table or checklist with: `action`, `owner`, `deadline`, `dependency`, `evidence`, and `status`. Use `Unassigned`, `No deadline set`, or `Not stated` rather than inventing missing values. Preserve dates exactly as spoken before normalizing them.

## Decision and risk pass

List decisions separately from actions. Capture reversed decisions, unresolved objections, assumptions, and risks that could block execution. Add open questions for every action whose owner, scope, deadline, or dependency is unclear.

## Verification

Before finalizing, compare each action and decision against the source. Quote high-impact commitments, deadlines, approvals, and changes of direction. Report uncertainty explicitly and never claim a task was agreed when the source only shows discussion.
